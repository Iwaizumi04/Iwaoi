A Letter For You

My Dearest, Alayna,

I'm not sure if I've told you this before but, A chuisle mo chroí. It is a saying in Irish; it means you are the pulse of my heart. I don't mean you are in my heart, no, you are the very beating of it. You are the blood flowing inside, systolic and diastolic action.

I can't begin to thank you and tell you how grateful I am that you are in my life. You add colour to my world, my life, and my soul. My very essence craves you like no other. Even if you're 500 miles away, my mind will remember and, if not, then my soul will.

I don't think it's coincidence we met. I believe that we've met before, in another time and another life. In every single one we have found each other, because no one else could fit the soulmate criteria like we do. Thank you for being in my life.

Forever yours.

— Kaitlyn
